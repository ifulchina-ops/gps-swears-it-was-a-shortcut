One line, in the GPS's own voice: its excuse for sending the driver down the wrong road. It blames the map — the map swore the lake was a road — and admits it was too embarrassed after three U-turns to confess the map lied. The entry is one line of 120 characters, inside the event's 1–200 character gate. `main.ts` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt`.
